MoonPrint

This interactive archive explores how global media reported the Apollo 11 moon landing in July 1969. It visualizes the geopolitical narratives of the Cold War, contrasting the triumphant tone. Through a dynamic visualization, the site compares journalistic narratives from different countries, highlighting variations in tone, language, and perspective across international publications. The project was created to mark NASA’s 70th anniversary of the West with the more subdued, technical coverage from the Soviet bloc.

Instructions:
• Click on map markers to view newspaper front pages.
• Select two markers to compare different perspectives.
